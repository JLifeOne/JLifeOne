⚠ Allergy Protocol

One guest (woman — confirm identity on arrival) has all four restrictions stacked. This is the highest single risk of the week. The protocol below runs every service.

Setup (do this once, Saturday)

  • Color-code: RED board, knife, tongs, skillet, sponge — used only for her food.
  • Dedicated pantry shelf labeled "ALLERGY — DO NOT CROSS-USE."
  • Brief any house staff: never re-plate, never swap, never garnish her dish.

Every Meal

  1. Cook her plate first, before the regular line is dirty.
  2. Plate it, cover with foil, label "Allergy — [Name]."
  3. Walk it to the table yourself. No house-staff handoff.
  4. If you touch pork/dairy/gluten/nuts mid-service, wash hands + change apron before touching her plate again.

Hidden Traps to Avoid

Gluten lurks in:
  • Soy sauce → use tamari
  • Jerk seasoning blends with breadcrumbs
  • Beer (Red Stripe ✗) → cider or GF beer
  • Malt vinegar
  • Curry powder fillers — verify brand
  • Hard-dough bread, bammy (use cassava bammy)
Pork lurks in:
  • Lard in some local pastry
  • Bacon fat sharing pan with eggs
  • Stock cubes (some contain pork)
  • Gelatin in some desserts → use agar
Dairy lurks in:
  • Butter finish on lobster → olive oil + lime
  • Cream in coffee — keep oat milk
  • Hollandaise → make olive-oil version
  • Cake batters — use coconut cream
Nuts lurks in:
  • Almond flour in GF baking → use sorghum/oat
  • Pesto, satay, mole, granola
  • Coconut is technically safe but always confirm with guest

Safe Default Builds

  • Protein: grilled fish, chicken, beef, goat, lamb (no jerk rubs unless verified GF)
  • Starch: rice & peas (coconut milk = naturally OK), boiled green banana, breadfruit, GF bread
  • Veg: callaloo, plantain, escovitch veg, all roasted veg in olive oil
  • Sauce: escovitch (vinegar/pepper/onion), mango salsa, fresh herbs + lime
  • Dessert: coconut sorbet, rum-soaked fruit, agar-set panna cotta with coconut
